Self-Stabilizing Spoon for Parkinson's Ailment

In this project, a stabilising spoon is being built for individuals with Parkinson's disease by using the concepts of sensor networks and the Internet of Things. The stabilising spoon senses any inadvertent tremors or shivers from the user and modifies its head appropriately, ensuring that the spoon's bowl stays stable at all times. A prototype was developed using a gyroscope to estimate angle in order to assist patients throughout the eating process.

Papers

1. Cost Effective Tremor Stabilization Spoon for Individuals with Parkinson's Disease

Parkinson's disease is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder characterized by involuntary hand tremors which substantially interfere with performing basic activities of daily living, especially eating. Patients frequently experience difficulty maintaining control of utensils, leading to food spillage and slowness during meals. Existing assistive utensils may be expensive or insufficiently responsive to varied tremor amplitudes and frequencies. Therefore, there is a need for a robust, cost effective, easily maintainable self stabilizing spoon device that can mitigate tremors in real time, thereby improving the eating experience for sufferers.

Status: In Progress
